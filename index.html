Brand position

Provable, not promised.

Vetro doesn't manage guards, patrols, or rotas. It answers one question with certainty: is this person legally allowed to work today? Built for the operator who has to answer to an ACS audit, a client due-diligence request, or their own conscience at 6am before a shift starts.

Vetro checks your officers' SIA licences and BS7858 vetting automatically, so you always know who's actually cleared to work — no more spreadsheets, no more chasing.
